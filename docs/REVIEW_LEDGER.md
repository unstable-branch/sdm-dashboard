## Disposition rules

No finding is closed by a branch name, test count, merged PR title, or a passing mock. A release claim requires the evidence in the next-gate column on the exact candidate SHA. Findings that depend on production credentials, deployment, governance, or public release remain separately approval-gated.

Original review strengths and rejected hypotheses remain context, not closure evidence. Preserve synthetic-only testing and do not copy private data, credentials, or local workspace details into this ledger.
